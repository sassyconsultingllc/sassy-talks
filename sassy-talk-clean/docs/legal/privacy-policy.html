Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

Sassy Consulting LLC ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Sassy-Talk push-to-talk application. This Privacy Policy explains, precisely, what data the app handles and where it goes. Sassy-Talk is built so that the people in your conversation are the only ones who can hear it: all voice is end-to-end encrypted, and we are structurally unable to listen to it, even when our servers help carry it.

The One Thing That Matters Most: Transports

Sassy-Talk can carry your voice over several different "transports." Which transport is in use determines whether any of your data ever reaches our servers.

Local transports — Bluetooth LE and Wi-Fi Direct / same-network.
When your devices reach each other directly, nothing is sent to our servers at all — not your audio, not your device ID, not connection metadata, not encrypted data, nothing. The conversation never leaves the local radio link or local network. We have no record that it happened.
Relay transport — internet fallback over our Cloudflare relay.
When your devices cannot reach each other directly (for example, you are on different networks across the internet), the app can fall back to a relay server we operate. The relay is a blind forwarder: every audio frame is end-to-end encrypted on your device before it is sent, the relay forwards only that ciphertext, and the relay cannot decrypt your audio. We never hold the keys. Audio is forwarded in real time and immediately discarded — it is never written to disk or stored.

In short: if the relay is not used, there is nothing on our server — encrypted or otherwise. If the relay is used, the only thing that passes through it is encrypted, opaque data plus the minimal routing information described below.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Device Name: A name you choose so others can recognize your device in a room. You can make it anything; it does not need to be your real name.
  • Audio Data: Your voice during push-to-talk. It is end-to-end encrypted on your device and is never stored. On local transports it never reaches us; on the relay it passes through only as ciphertext.

The relay does not require, and we do not ask for, your name, email, phone number, contacts, or location.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • The content of your conversations (we are cryptographically unable to read it)
  • Personal identification information (real name, email, phone number)
  • Location / GPS data
  • Contacts or address book
  • Usage analytics, behavioral tracking, or advertising identifiers

Encryption and Security

  • End-to-end encryption: Voice is encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Keys are established directly between participants using X25519 (Elliptic-Curve Diffie–Hellman) key agreement. The relay never receives the keys.
  • Blind relay: Our relay forwards ciphertext only and cannot decrypt it.
  • Capability tokens, not accounts: To open a relay connection, the app fetches a short-lived (5-minute), HMAC-signed token bound only to a room ID and an expiry. It contains no personal information and grants nothing beyond joining that one room.
  • No persistent storage of communications: Audio is never recorded or written to disk by the app or the relay.

Sharing an Invitation

When you share access to a room via a link, the invitation payload is encrypted on your device and the encrypted blob is stored briefly on our infrastructure (Cloudflare KV) so the recipient can fetch it. The decryption key is placed in the link's URL fragment (the part after #), which browsers and apps never transmit to the server. As a result, we hold only an encrypted blob we cannot read, and it expires automatically. Anyone you give the full link to can open the invitation — treat invite links like a key.

Push Notifications (Optional)

If you enable wake notifications, the app registers a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token with the relay, mapped to your room and peer ID, so that an incoming transmission can wake your device when the app is in the background. The push message carries only a room identifier so the app knows where to reconnect — never your audio or message content. FCM is operated by Google and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. If you do not enable wake notifications, no FCM token is registered and no push data is processed. You can disable this at any time in app settings.

Operational Logs

For debugging and abuse prevention, the relay may keep short-lived operational logs of connection events (a room being opened, a peer joining or leaving, rate-limit triggers). These logs contain connection metadata only — never the content of your audio, which we cannot read in any case. They are retained briefly and then rotated out.

Where Data Is Processed

When the relay transport is used, traffic is processed by Cloudflare, Inc., our infrastructure provider, on its global edge network. Cloudflare acts as our data processor. Optional push notifications are delivered by Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging). We do not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Self-Hosting

Organizations that require full data sovereignty can run their own relay. When the app is pointed at a self-hosted relay, none of the traffic described above touches Sassy Consulting's infrastructure — it stays entirely within the operator's environment.

Children's Privacy

Sassy-Talk is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your Rights

We do not maintain user accounts or profiles, so there is no stored personal record tied to you to access or delete. You can stop all data processing by leaving relay rooms, disabling push notifications, or uninstalling the app, which removes all local data from your device. Encrypted invitation blobs expire on their own; contact us if you need one invalidated sooner.
