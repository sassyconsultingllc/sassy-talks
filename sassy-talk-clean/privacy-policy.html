📱 Sassy-Talk: Privacy by Design

We minimize data collection and protect your communications.

Sassy-Talk is designed to work peer-to-peer (Bluetooth, WiFi multicast, or WiFi Direct) so audio is kept local whenever possible. The app also supports an optional encrypted "cellular relay" transport to let devices communicate over the internet when peers are not on the same local network. When the cellular relay is used audio frames are encrypted on-device (AES-256-GCM) and forwarded through a relay service (Cloudflare Durable Objects). The relay forwards encrypted audio but does not decrypt it. By default the relay is a transient forwarder and audio is not stored; some ancillary endpoints (contact form, downloads, or optional analytics) may collect minimal metadata — see sections below for details.

🚫 What We DON'T Collect (Unless You Use Optional Features)

By default Sassy-Talk does not collect identifiable conversation content. However, depending on the transport and optional features you use, the app may send minimal data to our backend endpoints:

  • ❌ We do not collect saved conversation recordings by default.
  • ⚠️ Cellular relay: Encrypted audio frames are forwarded via a relay service when you choose the "Cellular Relay" transport; the relay does not decrypt audio and does not persist audio by default.
  • ✅ Contact form: If you submit the contact form we will receive the name, email, and message you provide so we can respond.
  • ⚠️ Connection analysis / diagnostics: Some server endpoints may collect limited connection metadata (e.g., masked or hashed IP, country) to help diagnose connectivity issues; any stored logs are minimized and/or hashed where practical.
  • ❌ No contact list access unless you explicitly grant and use a feature that requires it.
  • ❌ No advertising IDs or third‑party advertising networks.

🔐 How It Works

[Your Microphone] ↓ (Capture voice) [Local Encryption - AES-256] ↓ (Bluetooth direct connection) [Peer Device] ↓ (Decryption) [Peer Speaker] ↓ (Instant discard - NO storage)

📋 Permissions Required

🎤 RECORD_AUDIO

Why we need it: To capture your voice when you press the Push-to-Talk button

What happens: Audio is transmitted directly to your peer via Bluetooth

Storage: Audio is NEVER saved anywhere

📡 BLUETOOTH (Connect, Scan, Admin)

Why we need it: To connect directly to your friend's device

What happens: Only paired device info is accessed (standard Bluetooth)

Data transmitted: Only encrypted voice to your paired device

🌐 INTERNET

Why it's declared: Used for optional features: the cellular relay (encrypted forwarding), contact form, download catalog, and payments (Stripe) if enabled.

Current usage: Internet is only used when you choose a feature that requires it — e.g., "Cellular Relay", submitting the contact form, or purchasing a product. Encrypted audio frames sent via the relay are forwarded but not decrypted by the relay.

Your privacy: When internet features are used we document what is transmitted and who receives it in the sections below.

🎯 Third-Party Services

Minimal third-party services are used for optional features.

When the cellular relay or server-backed features are used we rely on a small set of third-party services:

  • Cloudflare — Durable Objects & R2 for relay and optional downloads.
  • Stripe — payment processing for purchases (we do not directly handle card numbers in the app).
  • Email delivery provider — used to deliver contact form messages.

We do not include analytics SDKs or advertising networks by default.
